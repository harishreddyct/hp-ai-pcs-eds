Frequently asked questions

What is an AI PC?

An AI PC is a new class of computer designed with built-in AI capabilities. It includes specialized hardware like neural processing units (NPUs) to run AI tasks faster, smarter, and more securely—right on your device. HP OmniBooks and HP EliteBooks offer these AI capabilities.

What is the benefit of an AI PC?

AI PCs deliver faster performance, smarter user experiences, and better battery life by handling AI workloads locally. That means real-time enhancements without relying on the cloud.

What are examples of AI PCs?

Examples include HP Spectre and HP EliteBook models with Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen AI processors—built to support AI features like background blur, voice focus, and real-time language translation.

Who would need to use an AI PC?

Anyone who values speed, efficiency, and smarter workflows. From business professionals and creators to students and developers—AI PCs adapt to how you work and help you do more.

What are the key features of an AI PC?

Key features include an NPU for AI acceleration, enhanced security, longer battery life, and support for AI-powered apps like Windows Studio Effects and Copilot in Windows.

Can you run AI on your own PC?

Yes, but performance depends on your hardware. AI PCs are optimized to run AI tasks locally, while traditional PCs may rely more on the cloud—leading to slower or less secure experiences.

What is the benefit of local AI or AI on the edge processing power?

Local AI keeps your data on your device, improving privacy and responsiveness. It also reduces the need for constant internet access or cloud processing, saving time and energy.

How does an AI PC differ from a regular PC?

AI PCs include dedicated AI hardware like NPUs, enabling them to run AI tasks more efficiently. Regular PCs may not support these features or may rely heavily on cloud-based processing.

What are the main applications of AI PCs?

AI PCs power features like real-time transcription, intelligent noise cancellation, background blur, predictive typing, and personalized performance tuning—enhancing productivity and creativity.

Top brands of AI PCs?

HP is leading the way with AI PCs, alongside other top brands like Dell, Lenovo, and ASUS—all offering models with AI-ready processors and features.
